Tender-Ready Guidance — Council & NHS

Crucial rule: PPN 006 is triggered by a contract value over £5m per year — NOT your company’s turnover. Even a £1m–£2m turnover subcontractor can be required to provide a compliant Carbon Reduction Plan if sitting inside a Tier 1 supply chain.

PPN 006 Carbon Reduction Plans

Required for central government, local authority, and major public procurement tenders. Must be published on your website, director-signed, and commit to Net Zero by 2050.

  • Scope 1 & 2: Direct fuel, company vehicles, and purchased electricity/gas.
  • 5 Required Scope 3 Categories: Upstream transport, waste generated in operations, business travel, employee commuting, and downstream transport.

NHS Evergreen & Evidence Vault

NHS suppliers face escalating sustainability maturity thresholds (Level 1 minimum, higher for larger frameworks) alongside Net Zero, Modern Slavery, and Social Value commitments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need PPN 006 if my turnover is under £5m?

Yes, potentially. PPN 006 is triggered by the contract value (over £5m per year), not your company’s annual turnover. If you are a subcontractor in the supply chain for a £5m+ Council, NHS, or government contract, the Tier 1 contractor can require a PPN 006 Carbon Reduction Plan from you.

Do you work with property companies, private landlords, and estate agents?

Yes. We handle statutory accounts, landlord tax compliance, Capital Gains Tax planning on property disposals, and PPN 006 / carbon reporting packs where estate agents or property firms tender for public-sector frameworks.

Is tax planning guaranteed to save me money?

No ethical adviser can guarantee tax savings before reviewing your figures. We carry out legitimate, forward-looking tax planning in writing, scoped and quoted transparently upfront.

How does the carbon reporting process work with vsmecap?

You provide your electricity, gas, and relevant operational records. Proactive Consultancy advises and structures your submission using vsmecap, which extracts meter data, applies official UK DESNZ conversion factors, and generates a locked evidence pack for your tender.
